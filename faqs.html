Before you arrive

Questions, answered.

Scottsdale Airport

Most of what guests ask us comes down to three things: how to find the door, what to wear, and whether the jets really do roll past the window. The answers are below, along with the dining policies worth knowing before you sit down.

01

Getting here

Parking, the terminal and the view of the ramp.

Where exactly are you?
Inside the terminal at Scottsdale Airport, with the dining room and patio facing the taxiway. The address is 15220 N 78th Way, Scottsdale, AZ 85260. Map apps bring you to the terminal lot, which is where you want to be.
Is there parking?
Yes. Self parking sits directly outside the terminal doors and it is complimentary for guests. On busy evenings the lot fills, so give yourself a few extra minutes before your reservation time.
Do I have to go through airport security?
No. We are on the public side of the terminal. You walk in from the parking lot the way you would at any other restaurant. No boarding pass, no screening, no aircraft required.
Can we really see the aircraft?
That is the whole idea. The windows and the patio look straight out at the ramp, and jets taxi past through dinner. Window seats go first, so mention it when you book and we will do what we can.
02

At the table

Dress, reservations and how we handle the details.

What is the dress code?
Resort casual. Come in off the golf course or dress for the occasion, both read correctly in this room. We ask that swimwear stays at the pool.
Do I need a reservation?
It is the surest way to get the time and the table you want, and reservations can be made online through our booking page. Walk ins are welcome whenever there is room, and the bar is usually the best bet without notice.
How do large parties work?
For groups, call us at (480) 948-0101 so we can seat everyone together and talk through the menu in advance. Full buyouts and private gatherings are arranged through Volanti Hangar Events.
Are children welcome?
Very. Families come here specifically for the view, and a runway holds most kids through two courses. High chairs are on hand, just ask the host.
Can the kitchen handle allergies and dietary needs?
Tell us when you book and tell your server again when you sit down. Vegetarian, gluten and most allergy requests are routine for the kitchen when we know early.
Is the patio dog friendly?
Leashed dogs are welcome on the patio. Water bowls appear without being asked for.
Can I bring my own wine?
Corkage is available if you have a bottle you have been saving. Ask your server for the current fee. If you find yourself doing it often, a wine locker in the dining room is the better answer.
03

Beyond dinner

Lockers, helicopters, hangar events and catering.

What is a wine locker?
Your own storage in the dining room, holding your bottles at the right temperature until you arrive. Members keep wine here and have it waiting at the table. Full details sit on the Wine Lockers page.
How does the helicopter tour work?
It pairs a flight over the Valley with a table here, and it lifts off from the same airport you parked at. Timing and booking are covered on the Helicopter Tour page.
Can I host a private event?
Yes. Private dinners, receptions and full hangar parties are handled by Volanti Hangar Events, which has its own site and planning team.
Do you cater aircraft?
Volanti Jet Catering is our separate operation for departing aircraft, with its own menu and ordering. Crew and flight departments can order there directly.
What is Run the Runway?
A race held on the airport runway itself. Dates and registration live on its own site, linked in our menu at the top of the page.

Nowhere else in Scottsdale does a Gulfstream roll past the glass between the first course and the second.
