From the Journal

Opening Party For The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley The Palmeraie

Written at the hangar, Scottsdale Airport

Filed under Nights Out

In this note

  • 01 A new neighbor
  • 02 The evening itself
  • 03 Why we show up

Scottsdale and Paradise Valley get better every time somebody builds something worth driving to.

01

A new neighbor

A resort opened just down the road from us in Paradise Valley: The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, with The Palmeraie alongside it. We were invited to the opening party, so we traded the hangar for a valet line for one night.

Projects like this take years. You drive past the fencing, then past the cranes, then one evening the gates are open and there is music coming out of it. That first night has its own energy, and it does not repeat.

02

The evening itself

The desert does evenings well. The light goes long and orange across the mountains, the air finally cools, and the whole property turns into a party at the exact moment you stop noticing the heat. The Palmeraie was full of people walking, looking up, and taking it in.

We spent most of it talking to the people who built it and the people who will run it. Hospitality is a small world here. You end up shaking hands with half the room before the second drink.

03

Why we show up

Our dining room sits on the runway at Scottsdale Airport, which is its own kind of front row. Guests land, walk in, eat, and some of them go back out to a jet an hour later. A new resort up the road means more of those nights, for everyone.

So, congratulations to The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley and to The Palmeraie. We will be back when the party is over and the place settles into itself, which is usually the better visit anyway.

End of entry

The journal is where we keep the nights worth writing down: openings, long tables, and whatever rolls past the glass.
