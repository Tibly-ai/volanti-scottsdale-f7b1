Scottsdale Airport, Arizona

Dining on the runway.

VOLANTI SCOTTSDALE sits against the glass at Scottsdale Airport. Jets taxi past at eye level, the light goes gold over the field around dinner, and the kitchen keeps working straight through the evening traffic.

01

The room
faces the field.

Floor to ceiling glass, a patio on the airside, and tables set so that every seat gets the view. Guests arrive for lunch and stay through the afternoon departures. Pilots walk in from the ramp. It is the kind of restaurant where the room goes quiet for a moment when something loud lines up for takeoff.

Inside, the work is steady and unfussy: a kitchen built for a full dining room, a bar that pours properly, and a wine program with lockers along the wall for regulars who keep their own bottles here.

02

What happens here

  1. i.

    Lunch, dinner and the bar

    One kitchen, several menus across the day, plus cocktails and wine by the glass at the bar. The full lineup lives on the Menus page.

  2. ii.

    Private wine lockers

    Keep your own bottles at the restaurant, stored at temperature and waiting under your name when you walk in.

  3. iii.

    Helicopter tour

    Dinner on one side of the glass, a flight over the valley on the other. The tour departs from the field next to the restaurant.

  4. iv.

    Jet catering and hangar events

    Catering sent out to aircraft on the ramp, and private events staged in the hangar next door, from seated dinners to large receptions.

03

Finding us

We are on the airport side of the terminal area. Park out front, come through the lobby, and ask for the dining room. Tables by the glass go first on clear evenings, so call ahead if the view matters to you.
