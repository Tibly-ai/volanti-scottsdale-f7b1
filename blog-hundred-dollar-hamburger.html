Pilots have a name for a flight with no purpose beyond lunch. Here is why it still gets people out of bed before the heat comes up, and why it ends at a table by the glass.

No logbook required. Most of the room arrives by car and watches everyone else arrive the other way.

01

A burger that costs what the fuel costs

Somewhere along the way, general aviation gave itself a joke it has never let go of. You pick a clear morning, preflight an aircraft you probably do not need to fly that day, go somewhere an hour away, and order lunch. Add the fuel, the oil, the landing fee and the hangar time, and the cheeseburger on the plate carries a number no cheeseburger should carry. The hundred dollar hamburger.

Inflation has had its way with the figure. Nobody has bothered to rename it. The point was never the arithmetic.

The flight is the reason. The burger is the paperwork.

02

An excuse with a destination

Every pilot knows the restless version of a day off. The weather is good, the winds are light, and staying on the ground feels like waste. A destination fixes that. It gives the morning a shape: a route to plan, a field to study, a time to be back before the afternoon thermals build.

That is the whole trick of the hundred dollar hamburger. It turns an aimless hour of flying into a trip with an ending. And an ending matters, because the best part of any flight is the part where you shut down, climb out, and go sit somewhere with people who understand exactly why you came.

03

Ours happens to be at the field

VOLANTI SCOTTSDALE sits at Scottsdale Airport with the glass facing the runway, which makes it an unusually honest place to end a flight. Taxiing traffic goes by at eye level while plates come out. On a busy afternoon the conversation stops mid sentence because something interesting is rolling past the window, and nobody minds.

Fly in and the walk from the ramp is short. Drive in and you get the same view from the comfortable side of the fence. Either way, the hundred dollar hamburger has always been less about the price than about having a reason to go somewhere and a good table waiting when you get there.
