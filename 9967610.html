03 Before You Arrive

How booking
works here

We are a restaurant inside a working hangar, so a little planning goes a long way. Here is everything worth knowing before you drive over.

  1. 01

    Book the date, then the time

    Reservations run through our online system. Choose your party size first, since table availability shifts with the size of the group.

  2. 02

    Ask for the window

    Leave a note on your booking if you want to be close to the glass. We will do what we can with the floor plan that night.

  3. 03

    Large parties and private dining

    For a bigger group, a buyout, or an event in the hangar, call or email us directly instead of booking online so we can plan the room with you.

  4. 04

    If your time is gone

    Fully booked slots disappear from the calendar. Call the restaurant anyway. Plans change, and we can often work something out closer to the day. More answers live on our FAQs page.
